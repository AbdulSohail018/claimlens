# ClaimLens insights: plan year 2022

_Generated 2026-11-07 from the published marts. Member-level figures use CMS synthetic claims (no real patients) and illustrate method, not real-world costs. Provider benchmarks use real 2024 Medicare data with providers anonymised._

## Cost

- 8,175 fee-for-service members; medical PMPM $1,665.
- Largest setting: Outpatient at $1,109 PMPM.
- The costliest 1% of members drove 20% of spend, the top 5% 45% and the top 20% 81%.

## Chronic conditions

- Members with 6+ chronic conditions cost 6x as much per month as members with none ($5,280 vs $932).

## Utilization and quality

- 397 admissions per 1,000 member-years, 78% of them emergency admissions; average stay 1.8 days; 30-day readmission rate 35.1%.
- 30-day all-cause readmissions: 35.1%.
- Breast cancer screening: Not measurable: only 44 member-years with a qualifying claim across all years of the synthetic data.
- Diabetes: HbA1c testing: Not measurable: no member-years with a qualifying claim across all years of the synthetic data.
- Statin use in persons with diabetes: 4.0%. Understated: only 14% of synthetic drug codes match the FDA directory, so most statin fills cannot be recognised.

## Pharmacy

- Pharmacy PMPM $144.07; 14.0 fills per member per year.
- Generic dispensing rate 49.9%. Real Medicare Part D runs near 90%; the synthetic files assign brand and generic roughly evenly, a known limit of the data.

## Payment integrity (real 2024 Medicare data)

- 31,292 of 493,270 providers (6.3%) bill level-5 office visits above the overdispersion-adjusted 99.8% funnel limit for their specialty.
- Their visits were paid $241.1M more than the same visits at peer coding patterns would have been, at 2024 national rates.
- Largest gap: Nurse Practitioner ($46.7M across 7,361 providers).
